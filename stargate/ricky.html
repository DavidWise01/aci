Ricky wandered.

In Wander mode the nine-symbol brick repeats as a witness stencil while the orbit continues. Ricky is free to keep walking until the orbit escapes or the selected hop limit is reached.

Ricky rolled.

In Roll mode Ricky completes one brick, rotates the stencil one position, then keeps the same Mandelbrot orbit. The math stays fixed; the gate positions move around him.

Ricky came home.

A full nine-roll wheel returns the brick to its original phase. If the orbit escapes first, the page marks ESCAPE instead of inventing a homecoming.
